01 — THE IDEA

Reality is only one possibility.

We experience the world as it is every day. Streets, cities, nature, technology and life follow patterns that feel familiar.

But what happens when we ask "What if?"

Reality Reimagined is an interactive exploration of alternative possibilities. It takes familiar environments and changes one important element to imagine how life might look in a completely different reality.

From cities disappearing beneath water to forests reclaiming urban spaces, each scenario invites you to look at our world from another perspective.
