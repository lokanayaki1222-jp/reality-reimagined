FLOODED WORLD

Cities
Underwater

When the oceans rose, the city did not disappear all at once. First came the abandoned roads. Then the empty buildings. Eventually, water filled the spaces where people once lived.

Lina wakes inside the highest floor of an old apartment tower. Outside her window, boats move between skyscrapers. Streets have become rivers and rooftops have become islands.

She steps into the water and realizes that her old city is still there — only the world around it has changed.
